The 60-Second Pause

Control Driver Assessment

Rate each statement: 1 (Never) to 5 (Always)

Section 1: Fear (The Shield)
I worry things will fall apart if I don't oversee them.
I feel panic when I don't know my partner's plans.
Section 2: Rigidity (The Rules)
I have a "correct" way for every household task.
I get angry when plans change last minute.
Section 3: Pride (The Pedestal)
I believe I am more logical/capable than my partner.
I struggle to apologize even when I know I'm wrong.

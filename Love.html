Love Triangle Assessment

How to take this assessment:

  1. Choose Solo or Couple: In Couple mode, you will take turns privately.
  2. The Scale: Rate each statement from 1 to 5.
    • 1: Not at all / Never
    • 3: Moderately / Sometimes
    • 5: Completely / Always
  3. Privacy: If you are with your partner, the first person completes all 15 questions, then hands the device over. Your partner will not see your scores until the very end.

Relationship Map

How to read this graph:

The baseline (bottom line) starts at 0 in the center. Points are plotted from 0 to 10:

  • Commitment: Moves from 0 Right.
  • Passion: Moves from 0 Left.
  • Intimacy: Moves from 0 Straight Up.

The distance between the Red and Blue points on each line represents the "Gap" in your perspectives.
